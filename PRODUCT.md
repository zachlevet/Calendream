# Calendream

Calendream is a Today-first life-planning app for iPhone and web.

## Product thesis

**Today is where you live. The timeline is how you understand where your life is going.**

Today is a calm home for planning and completing the current day. It is not merely the closest zoom level of a calendar. From Today, a person can move outward into month, quarter, year, and long-term views that progressively summarize the same underlying life data.

## Core principles

1. **Today must be independently useful.** It contains the schedule, focus tasks, habit actions, quick capture, and reflection.
2. **Time has multiple valid precisions.** An item may belong to an exact time, day, month, quarter, year, or someday.
3. **Importance controls visibility.** An item's altitude determines how far outward it remains visible.
4. **The past matters too.** Moving backward should produce an understandable record of what happened, not just an archive of completed checkboxes.
5. **One source of truth.** Habit completion and its Today task represent the same occurrence.
6. **Local-first.** The app remains fast and usable offline; synchronization must never make local interaction feel remote.
